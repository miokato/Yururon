# Privacy Policy (Yururon for Even G2)

**Effective date: July 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer ("we") of **Yururon**,
an Even Hub app for Even Realities smart glasses (the "App"), handles
information in connection with your use of the App.

For the iOS version of Yururon, see its privacy policy
[here](/PrivacyPolicy/English). This page covers the Even Hub app for
smart glasses only.

## 1. About the Provider

The App is developed and operated by an individual developer.
For privacy inquiries, contact **support@yururon.app**.

## 2. Information We Collect

**The App does not collect any personal information.**

- There is no account registration or sign-in.
- We do not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.
- We do not serve ads or perform behavioral analytics (tracking).

## 3. Network Communication

To display paper summaries, the App fetches public data in read-only mode
from our backend (**Supabase**, `https://teutkgwmbxrztjznpwtj.supabase.co`).
No personal information is transmitted in this communication.

All communication is encrypted with TLS. For how Supabase handles
information, see the [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following only in local storage within the
Even Realities App, to provide its features. This data is never sent
anywhere.

- **Display language setting** (Japanese / English)
- **Paper feed cache** (for offline viewing)

## 5. AI-Generated Content

Paper summaries shown in the App are generated in advance by AI on our
backend. Only publicly available paper metadata (title, abstract, etc.)
is used as input to the AI.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as necessary. The latest revision date
is shown as the "Effective date" at the top of this page.

## 7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

**support@yururon.app**
